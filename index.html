Frequently asked questions

Do I need a full gym?
No. Programs can be built around a full gym, a home gym, or even minimal equipment (dumbbells + bands). We design around what you actually have access to.
How much time do I need per week?
Most clients train 3–4 days per week for 45–75 minutes. We can work with less if needed and still make meaningful progress.
What if I’m a complete beginner?
Perfect. Many clients start with little to no experience. The programming and form feedback are designed to build a strong foundation safely.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. All plans are month-to-month with no long-term contracts. You’re never locked in.
How do the check-ins and form reviews work?
You’ll send short videos of key lifts through a simple system. I review them and give specific feedback. Weekly check-ins cover how training and recovery are going so we can adjust in real time.
